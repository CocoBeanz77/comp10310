About Me

Hey there, I'm Archie. Eight years old and an excellent example of how far a cat can get in life with the right attitude.

I did not always have it so good in life. I was born on a farm down in Kerry, surrounded by dogs, horses, and big machines. It was rather chaotic but I must say it has made me into the friendly, confident, and unbothered cat I am today.

The interesting part is that I was never meant to go home with my humans on the day I was adopted. They had come all the way to pick up my brother Oscar, and one of his siblings for someone else in Dublin. As they were being led inside to see them, I saw an opportunity. I ran over and circled around their feet to draw attention to myself. Then I allowed Nicole to pick me up for a cuddle and that's when she fell in love (who wouldn't?).

While they were busy choosing a Siamese from the litter, the woman casually mentioned that she would throw me in for free. They laughed at first but then one of them asked if she was serious. She said yes! So I was promptly scooped up and placed in the carrier. A job well done.

On the drive back from Kerry, it was rather snug in the carrier since there were three of us in there, but we were all just small kittens so it wasn't that bad. During this drive is when I received my new name and left my old identity, Furby, behind.

Life these days is filled with luxury. I have endless toys, the humans boil prawns for me, and my only worry about is where the coziest spot to take my next nap is.
