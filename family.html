About My Family

I live in what I would describe as a very successful household, mainly because I am in it. Alongside me is the human who made this website (Nicole), her mum, her mum’s partner, and my granny, who moved in with us a while ago. There are a lot of people, which means I get a lot of attention, which I fully support.

I used to have another brother when I was younger, but one day he went out to play and never came back. Oscar was a Siamese cat who loved to eat human food. I don't like to think about it too much, but I will always remember him. After that it was just me for a while, running the place on my own, the undisputed ruler of the house.

Then my granny moved in after grandad passed away, and she brought her two cats with her. My new brother is alright, my new sister on the other hand seems to wake up every day and choose chaos. We argue. A lot. I try to be patient, but there is only so much one cat can do when faced with that level of attitude. Still, I remain the more im-paw-tant one in this household, and I think everyone knows it.

As for Nicole, she can get very...emotional. Every now and then she will get teary-eyed when thinking of me getting old one day, which is quite dramatic seeing as I am still in excellent shape. I like to remind her of this by screaming for food not 5 minutes after being fed, sprinting around the house for no good reason, and jumping on her when she's sleeping.
