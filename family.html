About My Family

I live in what I would describe as a very successful household, mainly because I am in it. Alongside me is the human who made this website, her mum, her mum’s partner, and my gran, who moved in with us a while ago. There are a lot of people, which means I get a lot of attention, which I fully support.

I used to have another brother when I was younger, but one day he went out to play and never came back. Oscar was a Siamese cat who loved to eat human food. I don't like to think about it too much, but I will always remember him. After that it was just me for a while, running the place on my own, the undisputed ruler of the house.

Then everything changed again when granny moved in after grandad passed away
